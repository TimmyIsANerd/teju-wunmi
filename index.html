Order of service

Please stand and sit as the minister directs

  1. 1

    Opening prayer

    2 minutes

    A short invocation as the congregation settles. The groom waits at the front with his best man.

  2. 2

    Processional hymn

    The bride's entrance

    Please stand as the bride is escorted down the aisle.

    Congregation sings

    To God Be the Glory

  3. 3

    Welcome and statement of purpose

    Minister

    Dearly beloved, we are gathered here together in the sight of God, and in the presence of these witnesses, to join together this man and this woman in Holy Matrimony; which is an honourable estate, instituted by God, and is not to be entered into unadvisedly, but reverently, discreetly, and in the fear of God. Into this holy estate these two persons present now come to be joined.

  4. 4

    Giving away

    Minister

    Who gives this woman to be married to this man?

    Father of the bride

    I do.

    He places the bride's hand in the minister's, who places it in the groom's.

  5. 5

    The charge to the couple

    20 minutes

    A brief word from Pastor Eric Joshua Oke on the meaning and responsibilities of marriage, spoken to the couple before they make their vows.

    Hymn

    God, Give Us Christian Homes

    B. B. McKinney

    Led by the choir. Please join in.

  6. 6

    Exchange of vows

    The couple repeat their vows after the minister.

    The groom

    I, Teju, take you, Wunmi, to be my lawful wedded wife, to have and to hold from this day forward, for better, for worse, for richer, for poorer, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, till death do us part, according to God's holy ordinance; and thereto I pledge you my faithfulness.

    The bride

    I, Wunmi, take you, Teju, to be my lawful wedded husband, to have and to hold from this day forward, for better, for worse, for richer, for poorer, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, till death do us part, according to God's holy ordinance; and thereto I pledge you my faithfulness.

    Hymn, sung before the rings

    O Perfect Love

    Dorothy F. Gurney

    Show the wordsHide the words
    1

    O perfect Love, all human thought transcending,
    lowly we kneel in prayer before thy throne,
    that theirs may be the love which knows no ending,
    whom thou in sacred vow dost join in one.

    2

    O perfect Life, be thou their full assurance
    of tender charity and steadfast faith,
    of patient hope and quiet, brave endurance,
    with childlike trust that fears no pain or death.

    3

    Grant them the joy which brightens earthly sorrow;
    grant them the peace which calms all earthly strife;
    grant them the vision of the glorious morrow
    that will reveal eternal love and life.

  7. 7

    Exchange of rings

    Minister

    These rings are the outward and visible sign of an inward and spiritual bond which unites your two hearts in endless love. As you receive this ring, and as you wear it, may it remind you always of the vow you have taken today.

    The groom

    With this ring, I thee wed.

    The bride

    With this ring, I thee wed.

  8. 8

    Pronouncement of marriage

    Minister

    Forasmuch as Teju and Wunmi have consented together in holy wedlock, and have witnessed the same before God and this company, and have declared the same by joining of hands and giving and receiving of rings; I pronounce that they are husband and wife together, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

  9. 9

    The kiss

    Minister

    You may kiss your bride.

  10. 10

    Prayer for the couple

    A short prayer of blessing over the new couple and their home.

  11. 11

    Presentation of the couple

    Minister

    Ladies and gentlemen, it is my joy to present to you, for the first time as husband and wife…

    Mr. and Mrs.Teju & Wunmithe latest couple in town

  12. 12

    Recessional hymn

    Please stand. The couple walk out together, followed by the bridal train.

    Congregation sings

    In Thanksgiving and Love

    Sir Jude Nnam

    Led by the choir. Please join in.

Please stay with us

The reception begins immediately after the service, here at the same venue. Take your seats, and let the celebration begin.

With love and thanks,Teju & Wunmi
